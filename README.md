# Wain Bil Iraq · وين بالعراق؟

**How well do you know Iraq?**

[**▶ Play now**](https://wain-bil-iraq.pages.dev/) · [العربية](README.ar.md)

Explore Iraq through a location-guessing game. Look for clues in the scene, choose a spot on the map, and discover how close your guess is to the real location.

[![Wain Bil Iraq — current home screen](media/home.png)](https://wain-bil-iraq.pages.dev/)

## Discover the game

- **Made for Iraq:** discover its cities, streets, scenery and landmarks.
- **Look for clues:** use signs, architecture and the landscape to place your guess.
- **Choose on the map:** zoom in and mark the location you think is correct.
- **Get your score:** compare your guess with the real location; closer guesses earn more points.

## How to play

1. Open [Wain Bil Iraq](https://wain-bil-iraq.pages.dev/) and select **العب هسة!** (Play now).
2. Study the scene and place your guess on the map.
3. Confirm your answer, check the distance and score, and continue through the rounds.

The game interface is in Arabic. Play directly in your browser with an internet connection.
